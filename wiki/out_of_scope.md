# Out of scope

What this wiki **deliberately does not cover**. Consumer agents should fall back to live retrieval (web, arxiv, semantic scholar, etc.) for these.

This list is load-bearing: the explorer skill reads it to avoid wasting cycles fetching sources outside scope.

## Universally out of scope

- Frontier results published in the last N weeks (live retrieval is better).
- Fast-moving SOTA leaderboards (changes faster than the wiki can re-verify).
- Novel investigation, hypothesis generation, experiment planning.
- Paper drafting, peer review, conference submission logistics.
